​Edition)​
​Event: Cymonic Campus Recruitment - Agentic Workforce Hackathon​
​Time Allotted: 5 Hours | Team Size: 4 Members​
​Core Philosophy: Dynamic Agentic Reasoning via Natural Language Parsing​
​1. The Core Strategy (Team-Wide Understanding)​
​We are building a Chatbot Interface (Streamlit) that ingests unstructured contractor​
​inquiries (e.g.,​​"Need a crane urgently tomorrow"​).​
​The agent will parse the text, calculate a Qualification Score (0-100) across 4 weighted​
​gates (Availability, License, Lead Time, Contractor Tier), and output one of three actions:​
​●​ ​✅ Auto-Quote (Score ≥ 80)​
​●​ ​⚠️ Request More Info (Score 50–79)​
​●​ ​🚨 Manual Review (Score < 50)​
